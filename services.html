Our Services

Tailored support for student mental well-being

1-on-1 Therapy

Private sessions with our experienced counselors to discuss any concerns or challenges you're facing. Our therapists specialize in supporting student mental health.

Duration: 50 minutes

🧘

Guided Meditation

Structured meditation sessions to help calm your mind, reduce stress, and improve focus. Perfect for beginners and experienced meditators alike.

Duration: 30 minutes

🎓

Academic Stress Counseling

Targeted support for managing academic pressure, exam anxiety, and balancing your studies with other aspects of life.

Duration: 45 minutes

Group Sharing Circles

Join fellow students in a safe, facilitated environment to share experiences and support each other through common challenges.

Duration: 60 minutes

Weekly Check-ins

Brief, regular check-in sessions to monitor your progress and adjust support strategies as needed.

Duration: 15 minutes

Well-being Workshop

Interactive workshops on various mental health topics such as stress management, healthy boundaries, and mindfulness practices.

Duration: 90 minutes
